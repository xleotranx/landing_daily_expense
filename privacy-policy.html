Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Version: 1.0.0

1. Introduction

Daily Expense ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Important: Daily Expense is designed with your privacy in mind. All your financial data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal expense data to any external servers.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Locally

  • Expense records and categories you create
  • Custom category names and preferences
  • App settings and theme preferences
All of the above data is stored exclusively on your device. We have no access to this data, and it is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

2.2 Voice Input

The App offers voice input functionality to add expenses quickly. Voice recognition is processed using on-device AI capabilities provided by your operating system (Android). We do not record, store, or transmit your voice data.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

We do not collect any analytics, usage statistics, or device information.

3. Permissions Used

  • Microphone: Used only when you choose to add expenses by voice. Voice data is processed locally and is not recorded or transmitted.
  • Storage: Used to export reports and data to your device. We do not access any files other than those you explicitly choose to export.

4. How We Use Information

Since all data remains on your device, we do not use your information for any purpose. The App functions entirely offline to provide you with:

  • Personal expense tracking and management
  • Expense categorization
  • Visual reports and charts
  • Data export functionality

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to any third parties. Your expense data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export and share it yourself.

6. Data Security

Your data is stored securely on your device. We recommend:

  • Using device lock (PIN, pattern, fingerprint, or face recognition)
  • Keeping your device's operating system up to date
  • Being cautious when exporting and sharing reports

7. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services: Required for app distribution and basic Android functionality
  • On-device Speech Recognition: Provided by Android for voice input features

These services are governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

10. Your Rights

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Delete any expense record at any time
  • Clear all app data through your device settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all stored data
